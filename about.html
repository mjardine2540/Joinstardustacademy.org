About us

Education that fits your life

Stardust Academy offers free and budget-friendly college courses and resources, so adults can further their education without student loan debt.

Our story

From the moment I graduated high school, getting into college felt out of reach. My biggest worry was taking on student loan debt that I'd be paying back for the rest of my life, and that was the last thing I wanted on my shoulders.

I know I'm not the only one. Many people finish high school and then face real barriers, like disabilities or mental health conditions that make it hard to keep a job. Others rely on SSI or SSDI and can only work limited hours without putting their benefits at risk.

That's why I created Stardust Academy: free and affordable courses, so you can keep learning and growing without debt and without risking the support you depend on.

Melissa Jardine

Founder, Stardust Academy

"When I graduated high school, the thought of carrying student loan debt for the rest of my life kept me from going to college. I started Stardust Academy so no one else has to make that choice."

Our mission

Affordable

Free and low-cost courses, so you can learn now and avoid student loan debt.

Flexible

Self-paced video lessons that fit around work, family and real life.

Supportive

Clear steps, encouraging lessons, and free resources for every stage of your journey.

Who we're for

  • Adults returning to school after a break, whether it's been two years or twenty
  • Working parents and anyone with a busy schedule
  • Low-income and subsidized-income learners who want to avoid debt
  • People living with disabilities or mental health conditions
  • People receiving SSI or SSDI who can only work part time
  • Anyone starting fresh, at any age
