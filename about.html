About us

Education that fits your life

Stardust Academy offers free and budget-friendly college courses and resources, so adults can further their education without student loan debt.

Our story

Too many adults are told, in one way or another, that college isn't for them. It costs too much. The paperwork is overwhelming. There's no time between work and family. Or it just feels too late.

Stardust Academy was created to change that. We believe everyone deserves a path to education, no matter their income, their age, or where they're starting from.

So we built something different: short, practical video courses you can watch at your own pace, free resources to support every step, and no FAFSA or financial aid forms standing between you and learning.

MJ

Melissa Jardine

Founder, Stardust Academy

"I started Stardust Academy to open doors and build confidence for adult learners. You belong in college, and you belong here."

Our mission

Affordable

Free and low-cost courses, so you can learn now and avoid student loan debt.

Flexible

Self-paced video lessons that fit around work, family and real life.

Supportive

Clear steps, encouraging lessons, and free resources for every stage of your journey.

Who we're for

  • Adults returning to school after a break, whether it's been two years or twenty
  • Working parents and anyone with a busy schedule
  • Low-income and subsidized-income learners who want to avoid debt
  • Anyone starting fresh, at any age
